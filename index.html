What we flag

Four signals. One shared language.

Every alert maps to a specific Smart Money Concept, with the zone, invalidation level and confluence factors laid out — not a black-box arrow.

Order Blocks

The last opposing candle before an impulsive move — marked as a zone, not a single price, so entries have room to breathe.

Zone-based

Liquidity Sweeps

A wick that grabs resting stops beyond an obvious high or low, then reclaims the level — the footprint of a stop-hunt in progress.

Reversal cue

Fair Value Gaps

Imbalanced candle sequences that price tends to revisit — used as a magnet target or a lower-risk re-entry zone.

Target / re-entry

Break of Structure

Confirmation that the prevailing trend has shifted — the point most SMC setups use to size and time an entry.

Confirmation

FAQ

Before you ask.

Is this financial advice?

No. DannyTrade highlights structure — order blocks, sweeps, gaps and breaks — for your own analysis. It does not place trades or guarantee outcomes. Trading in the securities and commodities markets carries risk of loss.

Which markets and instruments are covered?

NSE and BSE equities and indices, plus MCX commodities including gold, silver and crude.

How are alerts delivered?

In the app itself, in real time as structure confirms — this is a single-user tool with no accounts, subscriptions, or multi-channel delivery to manage.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no login and nothing to subscribe to — it runs for one user against your own connected data source.
